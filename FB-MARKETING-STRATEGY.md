# Facebook Ads Marketing Strategy — Aparna Kapur Real Estate

## Overview

This strategy uses Facebook and Instagram ads to drive targeted Vancouver homeowners and buyers to standalone landing page funnels at aparnakapur.com/lp/\*. Each funnel is designed to tease valuable real estate data (live MLS listings, market stats) and gate the full details behind a lead capture form — forcing a conversation with Aparna.

---

## Campaign Architecture

### Three Campaign Tiers

| Tier                     | Objective       | Budget Split | Audience                            |
| ------------------------ | --------------- | ------------ | ----------------------------------- |

| **Tier 2: Buyer Leads**  | Lead generation | 45%          | Active home shoppers                |

## Follow-Up System (Post-Capture)

This is where the slides' strategy kicks in. The landing page captures the lead. Everything below is Aparna's playbook.

### Day 0 (Immediate)

- **Auto-email** from website: Confirmation + "Aparna will reach out within 24 hours"
- **Email notification** to Aparna with full lead details + source tag

### Day 0-1 (Within 24 hours)

- **Personal call** using LPMAMA script:
  - **L** — Location: What area are you looking at?
  - **P** — Price: What's your budget range?
  - **M** — Mortgage: Pre-approved? With who?
  - **A** — Agent: Do you have an agent? (Assume no — they're on your landing page, not their agent's)
  - **M** — Motivation: Why are you looking now? Timeline?
  - **A** — Appointment: Let's get together. Send Calendly link.
- If no answer: **Personal text message** — "Hi [Name], this is Aparna. I saw you were looking at [context from source tag]. I have some great options for you. When's a good time to chat?"

### Day 2-3

- **Video text** (BombBomb style): Record a quick personal video holding a whiteboard with their name. "Hi [Name], I noticed you were looking at [area/price range]. I have a few properties I think you'll love. Let me know if you'd like to see them."

### Day 3-5

- **Follow-up call #2** with new information: "Hey [Name], just wanted to let you know [X new listings / price change / open house] just came up in [their area]. Thought of you."

### Day 7

- **Email** with curated listing selections based on their stated preferences

### Day 10-14

- **Final personal outreach**: If no response, one more call/text

### Day 14+ (No Response = Long-Term Lead)

- Move to **email drip** — monthly market updates for their neighbourhood
- **ZMOT principle** (Zero Moment of Truth): Be the last agent in their inbox when they're finally ready
- **Breakup message** (from the slides): "Hi [Name], I haven't heard from you so I'm assuming you've found a home. I'll stop sending property updates. If your situation changes, I'm always here. — Aparna"

## Tracking & Optimization

### Facebook Pixel Setup

- Install Facebook Pixel on aparnakapur.com (if not already)
- Set up **Custom Conversions** for each landing page form submission
- Create **Lookalike Audiences** from converted leads after 50+ conversions

### KPIs to Track Weekly

| Metric                       | Target                       |
| ---------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Cost Per Lead (CPL)          | Under $25                    |
| Landing Page Conversion Rate | 15-25%                       |
| Lead-to-Appointment Rate     | 10-20%                       |
| Ad Click-Through Rate (CTR)  | 1.5%+                        |
| Ad Frequency                 | Under 3.0 (avoid ad fatigue) |

### A/B Testing Plan

- **Week 1-2**: Run all ad variations, let Facebook optimize
- **Week 3**: Kill underperformers (CTR < 1%, CPL > $35)
- **Week 4**: Scale winners, create new variations
- **Monthly**: Refresh ad creative to combat fatigue
- **Quarterly**: Review lead-to-close conversion rates per campaign to double down on highest-ROI funnels

### Source Tag ROI Tracking

Every lead is tagged with its landing page source (e.g., "LP: Condos Under 750K"). When Aparna closes a deal, match back to the original source tag to calculate true ROI per campaign. This tells you which $200/month campaign generated a $15,000 commission.

## Launch Sequence

### Week 1: Foundation

1. Install Facebook Pixel + verify it fires on form submissions
2. Launch Campaign 1A (Home Value) + Campaign 2A (Condos <750K) — test with $30/day total
3. Monitor CTR and CPL daily

### Week 2: Expand

4. Launch Campaign 2D (New Listings) + Campaign 2E (Investment)
5. Start retargeting campaign (3A) once pixel has 100+ page views
6. Begin LPMAMA follow-up on all incoming leads

### Week 3: Optimize

7. Kill underperforming ads, scale winners
8. Launch Campaign 1B (Equity) + Campaign 2B (East Side)
9. Create Lookalike Audience from first 20+ leads

### Week 4: Neighbourhood Targeting

10. Launch 3-4 neighbourhood-specific campaigns (start with Oakridge, Kerrisdale, Kitsilano)
11. Full follow-up system operational (call/text/video/email drip)

### Month 2+: Scale & Iterate

- Add remaining neighbourhood campaigns
- Launch duplex and homes-under-2.2M campaigns
- Refresh all ad creative monthly
- Review lead-to-close data quarterly
