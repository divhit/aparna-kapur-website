Pricing Your Home

How I Price Your Home

I believe 80% of the marketing of your home takes place right now, when we determine the right price. In this market it is critical to establish a marketable price for your home.

To help us with this, I prepare a CMA, a comparative market analysis, which includes:

  1. Homes presently for sale in your neighbourhood. This is who our competition will be when we go to market.
  2. Homes recently sold in your neighbourhood. Unfortunately, you and I don't determine what homes are worth, the purchasers do. After looking at all the homes for sale suitable to them, they will decide which one is the best value. Recently sold homes give us true market value, not listings.

There are only two places to price your home

You can price it in line with other homes that are not sold, or you can price it in line with homes that have actually sold. If you were a willing buyer, which listings would you be more apt to consider?

My preference is to make sure you get the most activity, which generates the most offers, allowing you to retain the option of turning them down rather than have no offers at all. If we happen to get a low offer, my job is to negotiate the buyer up, rather than list at a price that may not attract any offers at all.

"When a new listing comes out, it attracts a great deal of attention and usually generates the most showings. If your home is not sold in 30 days, the educated buyers have decided the price is too high and have moved on to other listings."

Why the first 30 days matter most

Statistics show our chances of selling decrease dramatically after the first 30 days. That is why it is critically important to be priced competitively right out of the gate, so we never risk the largest pool of buyers overlooking your home in those first few weeks.

The Empowered Sale

How I Find Buyers For Your Home

A great price means nothing if the right buyers never see your home. Finding them is a deliberate strategy, not luck.

Most agents use an outdated process: putting a house up for sale and triggering private showings where buyers inconveniently kick the sellers out of the house and view the home in the complete absence of competition. This creates the all-too-familiar "buyer versus seller" mentality, where the buyer is constantly asking themselves "how much can I get from the seller?"

I do it differently. I use deep marketing channels to funnel all interested parties toward a heightened sense of competition. This proven process results in a "buyer versus buyer" mentality, where the buyer is constantly asking themselves "what do I have to do to beat the competition?" This is what I call the empowered sale, and it is precisely how I help my sellers achieve the best price.

How I reach the buyers

  • My active buyer database. I keep an ongoing list of qualified buyers searching Vancouver's south side, and work with buyers in the area directly.
  • "Just Listed" neighbourhood outreach. I let the immediate neighbourhood know a home has come up for sale, because the people who already love your area often know exactly who wants in.
  • Peer-to-peer marketing through fellow agents. I market your home to agents who have buyers right now, including off-MLS "sleeve listings."
  • Auto-email alert system. The moment your home matches a registered buyer's criteria, it lands in their inbox automatically.
  • The Oakwyn network. 900+ agents across BC, many with buyers actively looking in your neighbourhood.

From Decision to Keys

The Selling Process

Six clear steps. You always know where we are and what is coming next.

1
Step

The Decision to Sell

Before strategy, we talk about why. Upsizing, downsizing, relocating, unlocking equity. Your reason shapes every decision that follows. I'll give you a clear financial picture up front: capital gains, mortgage penalties, selling costs, and a detailed net sheet so you know exactly what you'll walk away with.

"In most cases I recommend selling first. It gives you negotiating power and removes the pressure of carrying two properties."

2
Step

Pricing Strategically

Pricing is a craft, not a guess. I prepare a thorough CMA covering recent comparable sales, your active competition, expired listings and what went wrong with them, days-on-market trends, and your home's unique features, then recommend the right strategy for your goals and the current market.

"Overpricing is the most common mistake sellers make. A home that sits develops a stigma. I'd rather price it right from day one, create urgency, and let the market compete for your property."

3
Step

Preparing Your Home

Buyers decide fast, often within seconds. I'll walk your home and give you a prioritized, ROI-focused plan: what's high-impact (declutter, neutral paint, clean fixtures, tidy landscaping), what's worth considering, and what is usually not worth doing before a sale. Then professional photography and full marketing bring it to market at its best.

"A weekend of decluttering and a few hundred dollars in touch-up paint can translate into tens of thousands at the offer table."

The Selling Process continued

4
Step

Offers & Negotiation

Every offer is more than a number. It is price, deposit, subjects, completion and possession dates, and inclusions. I analyze each as a complete package and manage multiple-offer situations with a clear, fair, structured process. This is where the right agent makes a measurable difference.

"The highest offer is not always the best offer. I once steered a client to a subject-free offer that was lower but clean. It saved them weeks of uncertainty and real risk."

5
Step

Inspections & Subject Removal

Once you accept an offer with subjects, the buyer has 5 to 10 business days for due diligence. I prepare you in advance by walking the home to identify anything an inspector is likely to flag, so we address what we can proactively and have responses ready for the rest. An optional pre-listing inspection can remove surprises entirely.

"No surprises means smoother negotiations. Informed sellers are much harder to push around."

6
Step

Closing & Moving Forward

When subjects are removed, you have a firm deal. I coordinate with both lawyers, your move, and every last detail through completion and possession. And my job doesn't end at the keys. Many of my clients work with me on both sides of a move, and I stay your realtor for life.

"By the time you get the keys, the hard work should be behind you. My job ends when you're settled and confident in what comes next."

Sell With Confidence

My Home Seller Protection Plan

One of the biggest fears in selling is being left without somewhere to go. These built-in protections make sure that never happens. As a real estate professional dedicated to providing the best possible service, I guarantee it.

Contingency 1. Upon receipt of an accepted offer the seller will have fourteen days to find another suitable property. If the seller is unable to find another suitable property the offer will be extended or become null and void.

Contingency 2. Upon receipt of an accepted offer the seller will have the option to rent back their own property at fair market rent for an agreed period of time to allow them to find another suitable property.

Contingency 3. Upon receipt of an accepted offer the seller will have the option to set the completion date no sooner than 3 months from the accepted offer to allow them to find another suitable property. If the seller is able to find a suitable property sooner, then upon mutual agreement the completion date can be moved ahead.

To Make Our First Visit Count

What I'll Need From You

A little preparation before our first meeting lets us move faster and price more accurately. Together we will build a "seller's homework book" for your home.

  • A spare key (and any garage remotes or fobs) for showings and access.
  • Recent utility bills (hydro, gas, water) so buyers can understand running costs.
  • Receipts & records for home improvements such as renovations, a new roof, windows, furnace, or hot water tank. These add real, provable value.
  • Your property survey or plan, recent tax notice, and title if you have it.
  • Strata documents, if applicable, such as recent minutes, the depreciation report, Form B, and bylaws.
  • Neighbourhood notes: nearby transit, schools, and what you love about the neighbours.
  • "Summer" photos if we're listing in winter. Garden, patio, and exterior shots taken in better weather help buyers see the home at its best year-round.
  • Your "10 Best Features" list, the worksheet on the next page. The things only you know about living here.

And from me, in advance

Before we meet, I'll send over copies of the key paperwork and sample documents so you can review everything at your own pace, with no pressure and no surprises. Bring your questions; that's what the first visit is for.
