From Decision to Keys

The Selling Process

Six clear steps. You always know where we are and what is coming next.

1
Step

The Decision to Sell

Before strategy, we talk about why. Upsizing, downsizing, relocating, unlocking equity — your reason shapes every decision that follows. I'll also give you a clear financial picture up front: capital gains, mortgage penalties, selling costs, and a detailed net sheet so you know exactly what you'll walk away with.

"The most common question I get is whether to buy or sell first. In most cases I recommend selling first — it gives you negotiating power and removes the pressure of carrying two properties."

2
Step

Pricing Strategically

Pricing is a craft, not a guess. I prepare a thorough Comparative Market Analysis — recent comparable sales, your active competition, expired listings and what went wrong with them, days-on-market trends, and your home's unique features — then recommend the right strategy for your goals and the current market.

"Overpricing is the most common mistake sellers make. A home that sits develops a stigma. I'd rather price it right from day one, create urgency, and let the market compete for your property."

3
Step

Preparing Your Home

Buyers decide fast — often within seconds. I'll walk your home and give you a prioritized, ROI-focused plan: what's high-impact (declutter, neutral paint, clean fixtures, tidy landscaping), what's worth considering, and what is usually not worth doing before a sale. Then professional photography and full marketing bring it to market at its best.

"A weekend of decluttering and a few hundred dollars in touch-up paint can translate into tens of thousands at the offer table. I'll give you a specific plan for your property — not generic advice."

From Decision to Keys

The Selling Process continued

4
Step

Offers & Negotiation

Every offer is more than a number — price, deposit, subjects, completion and possession dates, inclusions. I analyze each as a complete package and manage multiple-offer situations with a clear, fair, structured process. This is where the right agent makes a measurable difference.

"The highest offer is not always the best offer. I once steered a client to a subject-free offer that was lower but clean — it saved them weeks of uncertainty and real risk."

5
Step

Inspections & Subject Removal

Once you accept an offer with subjects, the buyer has 5–10 business days for due diligence. I prepare you in advance — walking the home to identify anything an inspector is likely to flag — so we address what we can proactively and have responses ready for the rest. An optional pre-listing inspection can remove surprises entirely.

"No surprises means smoother negotiations. Informed sellers are much harder to push around."

6
Step

Closing & Moving Forward

When subjects are removed, you have a firm deal. I coordinate with both lawyers, your move, and every last detail through completion and possession. And my job doesn't end at the keys — many of my clients work with me on both sides of a move, and I stay your realtor for life.

"By the time you get the keys, the hard work should be behind you. My job ends when you're settled and confident in what comes next."

A note for buyers, too

Many sellers are buying as well. I guide clients through a parallel six-step buying process — from clarifying your reasons and financial preparation, to finding the right home, due diligence, and closing day. If you're moving up, down, or across town, we can plan both sides together. Just ask.

To Make Our First Visit Count

What I'll Need From You

A little preparation before our first meeting lets us move faster and price more accurately. If you can gather these, our time together goes much further.

  • A spare key (and any garage remotes / fobs) for showings and access.
  • Recent utility bills — hydro, gas, water — so buyers can understand running costs.
  • Receipts & records for home improvements — renovations, new roof, windows, furnace, hot water tank. These add real, provable value.
  • Property documents — title, recent property tax assessment, and any survey or plans you have.
  • Strata documents (if applicable) — recent minutes, depreciation report, Form B, bylaws.
  • Warranties & manuals for appliances and major systems.
  • "Summer" photos if we're listing in winter — garden, patio, and exterior shots taken in better weather help buyers see the home at its best year-round.
  • Your "10 Best Things" list — the worksheet on the next page. The things only you know about living here.

And from me, in advance

Before we meet, I'll send over copies of the key paperwork and sample documents so you can review everything at your own pace — no pressure, no surprises. Bring your questions; that's what the first visit is for.
